# Mercury Redux — Bug-typed Delta Flygon artwork source scan
_Date: 2026-11-09; research-only; no artwork selected or imported_

## Exact user requirements
- A genuinely differentiated, cohesive **Delta Trapinch → Delta Vibrava → Delta Flygon** family, not color changes to standard art.
- Delta Trapinch: includes **Bug** typing (mono Bug or dual).
- Delta Vibrava: includes **Bug** typing (mono Bug or dual).
- Delta Flygon: **Bug/Ground** (fixed).
- Prefer sourced, original-quality DS-compatible battle pixel art, ideally normal front/back, shiny front/back and a distinctive icon for **each** stage.
- A normal Trapinch/Vibrava/Flygon family and **one regular Mega Flygon** remain separate. Do **not** reinsert Electric/Dragon Redux B or its Mega.
- Preserve source files, historical approvals, licenses, author credits and stage-by-stage user design approval.
