Most of the sprites are made from scratch by NanaelJustice. 
Some icons (Venonat, Oddish, Gloom, Vileplume, Mimiegg and Eggvour) are edits from the official gen 6 game sprites. Oddish front sprite is also an edit from it's gen 4 sprite.
Earthrethian Incineroar's colors comes from leParagon (Gen7 Sprite Project)'s Incinerorar front sprite. Animon's colors also comes from LunaMaddalena (FireRed extravaganza 2.0 beta alpha prerelease)'s Animon front sprite.
Gorochu's design interpretation was made by Foxeaf.
